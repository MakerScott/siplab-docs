# Tags

Tags are labels we put on a drink after the recipe is written. You do not pick them when you create. We read the ingredient list, the [nutrition](nutrition.md) panel, and the name and description, then attach the chips that fit.

You can tap a chip on a drink to filter the feed. You can also open **Tags** on the drinks page and pick more than one. A drink has to match every tag you pick.

{% hint style="info" %}
We only look at the ingredient list for diet tags, not a mention of milk or honey in the steps.
{% endhint %}

## How Tags Get on a Drink

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### The recipe finishes first

Tags wait on the locked ingredient list and the nutrition panel. A missing panel means we skip High Protein, Low Calorie, and Low Sugar so an empty estimate does not mark a drink Low Calorie.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### We read the list and the panel

Lifestyle tags come from what is in the glass and what the panel says. Vegan, Dairy Free, Gluten Free, and Nut Free come from ingredients. High Protein, Low Sugar, Low Calorie, and Caffeine Free come from the numbers. See [Nutrition](nutrition.md) for how those numbers are built.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### We read the name and description

Benefits and seasons come from ingredients plus the words on the drink. If you asked for a calming lavender tea, Calm can land even before we see the panel. If the name says pumpkin spice fall latte, Fall can land too.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### The chips show on the drink

The recipe goes live with its tags. Some diet chips stay hidden until you set **Dietary Preferences** on your profile. Filtering still uses the tags we assigned, even when a chip is hidden on the card.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}

{% endhint %}

## Seasonal

Summer, Fall, Winter, Spring, and Holiday land when the name, description, or steps mention that season or holiday. Halloween can be both Fall and Holiday. Christmas, Hanukkah, and festive copy can land Holiday.

## Filter the Feed

On the drinks page, open **Tags**. The menu is grouped as Lifestyle, Benefits, and Seasonal. You can search the list, tap chips on or off, and click **Clear**.

Tap a chip on a drink to jump to drinks that share that tag. Combine tags when you want a narrower list, such as Vegan and High Protein together. Every selected tag has to be on the drink.
