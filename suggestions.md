# Suggestions

**Try adding** chips offer the next ingredient while you are still describing the drink. Tap a chip and that line drops into your description. You can ignore the chips and type your own line. Amounts you type stay put when the recipe is written.

The same chips appear when you [create](README.md) a drink and when you [remix](remix.md) one. A [model](models.md) picks the ideas. The recipe is written later, on the usual path.

## What a Chip Says

Each chip is one short kitchen line, such as “1 cup oat milk” or “1 banana.” The amount stays on the chip. Milk is named by type, such as whole milk or oat milk. When a flavor is sold as more than one form, the chip names the form, such as ube syrup.

You see up to six chips on a wide screen. On a phone, three show at a time. Tap one, and another idea can move into the open spot.

The round button beside the chips asks for a new set. That set skips the chips already on screen. While a new set loads, the button spins.

## How They Stay in Step

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### A category comes first

On create, you pick Hot Coffee, Iced Coffee, Hot Tea, Iced Tea, Juice, or Smoothie. On remix, we keep the category of the drink you opened. Ideas follow that category. A hot coffee starts with coffee ideas. A smoothie starts with smoothie ideas.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### An empty box gets starters

If you have not typed anything yet, the chips are strong starters for that category and for hot or cold. Focus the box on create and they show up. On remix, the box already holds their ingredient list, so the chips suggest what to add next.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Your lines steer the next ideas

After you type, or after you tap a chip, the next ideas follow what is already in the box. They should finish this drink. A chip that repeats a line you already wrote, including a close synonym such as oat milk and oatmilk, is left out. Ideas update after a short pause in your typing.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Water stays rare

Plain water is skipped unless the method truly needs it. Ice is fine on a cold drink.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Dietary Preferences

If you are signed in and you have set **Dietary Preferences** on your profile, every chip has to fit all of them. Guests, and signed-in people with none set, get ideas with no diet filter.

The same diet meanings are on [Tags](tags.md). For these chips, that means:

- **Vegan.** No dairy, honey, eggs, collagen, or gelatin.
- **Dairy Free.** No milk, cream, butter, whey, cheese, or yogurt. Plant milks are fine.
- **Gluten Free.** No wheat, barley, rye, malt, or oats.
- **Nut Free.** No almonds, cashews, peanuts, hazelnuts, walnuts, pecans, pistachios, macadamias, or their milks and butters.
- **Caffeine Free.** No coffee, espresso, matcha, caffeinated tea, cacao, cocoa, guarana, or mocha. Decaf and herbal tea are fine.

Turn on more than one, and a chip has to satisfy every preference you turned on.
