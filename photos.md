# Photos

Every new drink gets a still of the finished pour. A [model](models.md) paints it from the recipe, not from a camera in a kitchen. The recipe is already public while the overlay says **Building Drink**.

{% hint style="info" %}
The picture is a catalog still, about 4:3. A [video](video.md), when one exists, is a separate phone-tall clip made from this photo.
{% endhint %}

## How the Photo Is Made

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### The recipe goes live first

You land on the drink while the photo is still building. The name, ingredients, steps, and [nutrition](nutrition.md) are already there. Remix starts a new photo the same way. The original drink keeps its picture.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### We read the finished recipe

The model sees the name, category, ingredients, steps, and whether the drink is hot or cold. It has to mix a liquid color those ingredients would actually make. An oat milk latte should look like oat milk and espresso, not a generic brown.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### We pick one glass

Hot drinks stay in one unbranded opaque ceramic mug, handle and all. Cold drinks stay in one unbranded glass, with no handle anywhere in the frame. The vessel is one material all the way around. We do not switch to a mason jar, a to-go cup, or a second glass for the camera.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### We pour it the way you would serve it

The drink sits in the center and fills most of the frame, about three-quarters full. Window light keeps the liquid color honest.

- Hot: visible steam, no ice, no condensation
- Iced: clear ice and condensation, no steam
- Blended: thick and just poured
- Shaken: frothy, with a wet rim
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Garnish only if the recipe called for it

Whipped cream, cold foam, a mint sprig, or a cinnamon dusting land only when the method says to finish that way. If there is no finish, the top stays plain. Leftover ingredients may peek at the soft edges of the counter. They stay small and out of focus so they do not shrink the drink.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### It lands on the drink

The still replaces **Building Drink**. If the photo fails, the recipe stays live. You will see that we could not generate a photo.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What the Photo Will Not Show

- People, hands, faces, or a second drink
- Logos, store bottles, product labels, or printed packaging
- Straws, overlay text, or captions
- A chopped garnish pile or a fruit ring around the rim
- A blank studio or a wide table that makes the drink tiny
- A generic smoothie purple, neon green, or jet-black coffee unless these ingredients would actually look like that

Kitchen tools stay in the far blur and appear once. Anything that holds an ingredient is plain unlabeled kitchenware.
